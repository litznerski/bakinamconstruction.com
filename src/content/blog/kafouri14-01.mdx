The new house in Kafouri 14 was still under construction when the war started in April 2023. There was not much to steal or vandalize.
When we returned we found all the electrical wiring stripped and stolen.
Because the house is on a corner plot near to an electrical company the bullet and shrapnel holes in the walls witness the fighting in this area.
