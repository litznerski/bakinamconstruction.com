Standing on that open plot of sand, it took real imagination to see what would soon become a full family home. Building in Khartoum requires patience, trusted master builders, and daily supervision. That first visit set off a decade of deep local experience, learning the land, sourcing materials, and understanding what quality construction truly demands in this environment.
